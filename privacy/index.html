Privacy policy.

This directory is built to make finding phone repair services easier — here's how we handle the limited information we collect along the way.

What we collect

Browsing the directory itself doesn't require an account or any personal details. If you submit the "list your shop" form, we collect what you enter — business name, contact name, email, phone number and suburb — so we can review and add your listing.

How we use it

Information submitted through the listing form is used only to review, add and maintain your shop's listing on this directory. We don't sell or share it with third parties for marketing.
