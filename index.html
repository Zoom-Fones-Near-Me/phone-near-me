Questions, answered.

How much does a phone screen repair cost in Sydney?

Most screen repairs range from $89 for older models to $350+ for the latest flagships. Prices vary by model and whether you choose original or high-quality aftermarket parts.

How long does a repair take?

Screen and battery repairs are usually done while you wait — 30 to 60 minutes. Water damage and board repairs can take one to two business days.

Will I lose my data?

Standard repairs like screens and batteries don’t touch your data. We still recommend a backup before any repair.

Do repairs come with a warranty?

Most listed repairers offer a 3 to 12 month warranty on parts and labour. Check each shop’s listing for details.

Is it cheaper than going to the manufacturer?

Independent repairers are often 30–50% cheaper and faster, especially outside warranty.
